# Building an Information Portal for a Financial Services Business

A business in the financial sector, focused on payments, wanted one central place to publish news, guides and expert content for people trying to understand how payments work, from card schemes to paying household bills. We built them a portal their own team could run day to day, and it now publishes an average of three articles every week.

## The challenge

The client had a lot of knowledge to share but nowhere good to share it. They needed a site that could:

- Publish articles regularly without needing a developer every time
- Show the latest content clearly, so return visitors could see what was new
- Build trust in a sector where readers are careful about who they listen to
- Have room to grow into an academy style learning area later on

## What we built

We looked after the whole project, from the brand to the website to keeping it all running.

**Logo and branding.** We designed the logo and helped shape the wider brand, giving the business a professional look that builds trust with readers.

**A simple homepage.** It leads with a clear welcome message and a feed of the latest articles, so fresh content is always the first thing visitors see.

**Easy publishing.** The client's team can write, schedule and publish articles themselves, each with a date, a summary and a "Read more" link.

**SEO.** We set up the site structure, headings and article layout with search in mind, then put a dedicated SEO strategy in place to keep growing visibility over time.

**Hosting and email.** We host the website and the business email inboxes, so the client has one trusted partner for everything and no technical headaches.

**Room to grow.** The platform supports both the news portal and a future academy, so the business can move into training without rebuilding the site.

## The result

- An average of three articles published every week
- Traffic grew steadily month on month
- Search rankings improved thanks to a dedicated SEO strategy
- The client runs their own content without relying on outside help for everyday updates

As the client put it:

"Red Cherry IT is an excellent media partner who we rely on for their trusted judgement and honest advice."

## Why this approach works for content led businesses

In a sector like finance, readers are careful about who they listen to, so a site has to look credible and make it easy to see that real, current expertise is behind it. A clear feed of fresh articles does both jobs at once. Just as important, the publishing process has to be simple enough that the team actually keeps doing it. A portal that needs a developer for every post tends to go quiet, while one the team can run themselves keeps building visibility week after week.

## FAQs

**Can a business publish its own articles without a developer?**
Yes. This site was built so the client's team can write, schedule and publish articles themselves, including the date, summary and "Read more" link for each one.

**Can a website like this grow into a training or academy area later?**
Yes. The platform was set up to support both the news portal and a future academy, so the business can add training content without rebuilding the site.

**Do you handle hosting and email as well as the website?**
For this project, yes. We host the website and the business email inboxes, so the client has one partner to deal with rather than several.

**Is this only relevant to financial services businesses?**
No. Any business with expertise to share, in any sector, can benefit from a site that makes publishing easy. It is one of the best ways to build trust and get found online.
