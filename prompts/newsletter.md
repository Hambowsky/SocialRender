Write the {month} Red Cherry IT newsletter. It goes to clients, past enquiries and people who signed up on {site} for practical website and marketing tips. David reads, edits and approves it before anyone gets it.

Anything to focus on this month: {focus}

New guides on the website (title, summary, link):
{articles}

A client story you may use:
{case_study}

Write:
* subject: under 60 characters, specific and useful, no clickbait, no emoji.
* preheader: one sentence under 100 characters that adds to the subject, shown in the inbox after it.
* body: Markdown, 250 to 400 words, in this order:
  1. A short hello from David, two or three sentences, on what this issue covers. Start with "Hi," on its own line.
  2. "## New on the blog": two or three of the guides above, each a bullet with the title as a link and one line on why it is worth reading.
  3. "## Quick tip": one practical thing a business owner can do this month on their website or Google listing, in a short paragraph.
  4. "## Never miss an enquiry" (or a better heading in the same spirit): one of our AI lead tools that fits the season or the focus, what it does for them in plain words, with a link to its page. No price.
  5. "## Recent work": the client story in two sentences, with a link to {site}/case-studies.
  6. A one line sign off, then "David" on its own line.

Rules:
* Links only to {site} pages listed here or in what we offer. Never invent a page or a fact.
* Write to one reader as "you". Friendly and direct, like an email from someone they know.
* No unsubscribe line or footer: the email template adds those.
