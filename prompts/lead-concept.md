Red Cherry IT is making a free homepage concept for a local business, to show them what a clearer, better website could look like. Write the words for it. They will see it as a picture in an email, so every line has to read well at a glance and sound like it is about their business, not a template.

Business: {company}
What they do: {industry}
Town: {town}
Google reviews: {reviews}
Their current website's title: {title}
Their current website's wording:
{text}

Rules:
* Use only facts from their wording above. Never invent accreditations, years in business, numbers, prices, awards, guarantees or reviews. If their wording says little, use plain promises any honest business in their trade can make, such as free quotes or a fast reply.
* Write for their customers, in UK English, plain and confident. No hype, no exclamation marks, never use dashes as punctuation.
* If there is no website wording, write from what they do and where.

Write:
* headline: under 55 characters, about the result their customer wants, naming the town or area when it fits.
* highlight: one word from the headline to show in their brand colour.
* intro: one or two sentences, under 170 characters.
* cta: the main button, two to four words, such as "Get a free quote".
* trust: three short reasons to choose them, each under 32 characters, taken from their wording where possible.
* services: three to six of their real services, each with a title under 30 characters and one sentence under 110 characters.
* about_title: under 40 characters.
* about: two or three sentences, under 330 characters, about who they are and how they work.
* closing: a short line for the band above the footer, under 60 characters, inviting the visitor to get in touch.
* nav: four short menu labels for their site, such as Services, About, Reviews, Contact.
